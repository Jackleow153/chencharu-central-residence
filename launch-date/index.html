Chencharu Central Residences / Launch date

Launch date

End of January 2027 is a reported window, not a booking day.

The ebook cover says “launching end January 2027”. It does not name a preview Saturday or a booking day. Pages that do are not consistent with each other.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

Timeline that is on paper

  1. 26 Sep 2024Tender launched HDB put Chencharu Close out with the Sembawang Road executive condominium site. Confirmed
  2. 11 Sep 2025Tender closed, three bids Top bid about $1.01 billion. The Business Times reported the result the same afternoon. Confirmed
  3. 26 Sep 2025Awarded HDB accepted Evia MCS, Gamuda (Singapore) and H108 at $1,012,588,989.89. Confirmed
  4. 29 Sep 2025Gamuda announcement Letter of award dated 26 September. The land is to be held through Polaris Times Square Residences Pte Ltd and Times Square Mall Pte Ltd. Confirmed
  5. 24 Dec 2025Final land payment fell due Gamuda’s filing set 75% of the price, $759,442,000, due on this day. Confirmed The lease starts when the government receives the full price, and it excludes the bus-interchange and hawker-centre land. The ebook prints 99 years from this date. No receipt was in the documents checked here. Reported
  6. End Jan 2027Ebook launch window Not a published developer sales date. Reported
  7. Sep 2032End of HDB’s 84-month completion period Counted from acceptance on 26 September 2025. This is the contractual period for the project, not a residential TOP date. No TOP was found on the HDB award page. Confirmed

Dates this guide is not adopting

A 16 January 2027 preview and a 30 January 2027 booking day appear on one agent site and are marked TBC. Another site says the preview has not been announced. Completion years of 2029 and 2030 appear on other agent sites without an HDB source. They are not used as the date of this project.
