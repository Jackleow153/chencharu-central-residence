Updates

What changed, and what did not.

As of 18 November 2026 there is still no developer price list, no official floor plan and no confirmed showflat address.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

  1. 18 Nov 2026HDB’s June 2024 precinct plan was checked against CNA The 400-metre bus road and the bungalow at 50 Bah Soon Pah Road are in this guide as HDB’s plan. The ebook’s 5,200 flats is not used as a total. There is still no developer price list.
  2. 18 Nov 2026Gamuda’s seven-page Bursa filing was read in full The land companies are owned 50% by Gamuda (Singapore), 30% by Evia MCS and 20% by H108. The floor-area minimums, the $979.84 psf ppr rate and the 24 December 2025 final-payment date are now in this guide.
  3. 4 Nov 2026HDB launches Chencharu Acres A fifth Chencharu BTO, in the November 2026 exercise. CNA reported the launch. Confirmed Write-ups put the project at 1,582 flats. That count is not added into a precinct total here. Reported
  4. 11 Dec 2025HDB publishes the Chencharu environmental study About 7 hectares along Yishun Avenue 1. No area of high ecological value. Feedback ran to 7 January 2026. Confirmed The report expects clearance in the third quarter of 2026, after further approvals. The Straits Times said late 2026. Reported
  5. 17 Nov 2026The ebook was checked against HDB, Business Times and LTA The 13-stop claim and the corridor timing were not adopted. Guide quanta stay labelled as guide quanta.
  6. 16 Nov 2026PropNex ebook file date Unit table, guide quanta, facilities list, end-January 2027 window. Reported
  7. Sep 2026LTA revises the North-South Corridor Viaduct phases from 2029. Tunnel to the ECP from 2031. Confirmed
  8. 26 Sep 2025HDB awards Chencharu Close $1,012,588,989.89. Confirmed
  9. 11 Sep 2025Three bids reported Second bid $845 million. Knight Frank comments above $2,300 psf. Confirmed

A new row belongs here on the day a developer price list, a floor plan or a showflat address is published. Not before.
