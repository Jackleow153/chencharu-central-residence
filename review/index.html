Chencharu Central Residences / Review

Review

Chencharu Central Residences review: worth shortlisting for own-stay, not worth pricing off a guide quantum.

Buyers who want daily uses under a new private home in Yishun have a real product here. Buyers who need a number to underwrite should wait. The land fact is solid. The selling price is not.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

What holds up

  • HDB required the interchange, the hawker centre and the shops. This is not a slogan added later.
  • The land rate is about $980 psf ppr, and the top bid cleared the second bid by about 20%.
  • Khatib is on the North-South Line, one stop from Yishun and Northpoint City.
  • It is the first private-housing GLS site in the Chencharu precinct.

What does not

  • No developer price list, no floor plans, no confirmed showflat.
  • 875 and about 860 are both in the record.
  • The ebook’s 13 stops to Raffles Place, and its North-South Corridor timing, are wrong against LTA.
  • HDB’s completion period runs to September 2032. You are buying a long build on a working interchange site.

Who it suits

An owner-occupier in the north who wants a new 99-year condo, can live with a 2030s completion, and cares more about the hawker centre, the buses and Khatib than about being the first resale in the precinct. The family sizes are the bulk of the reported mix: 395 three-bedroom homes and 180 four-bedroom homes.

It is a weak fit if you need a psf you can defend this month, if you want a shop as part of the purchase, or if you are treating an agency guide price of “from about $1.3 million” as a cheque amount. The retail is one strata lot. You are buying a home.

Risks to price in, not to skip past

  • The second bid was $845 million. The winner paid about $168 million more. That premium has to come back in the selling price or in a reading of the podium that the under-bidders did not share.
  • Marcus Chu, CEO of ERA, said on 11 September 2025 that the land rate reflects the hawker centre and bus interchange, not a cheap Outside Central Region plot. He also said the cost of building those two uses is likely to be reimbursed. That reimbursement is his comment. It is not a sentence in the Gamuda filing. Reported
  • The lease starts when the government receives the full land price. Gamuda set the final 75% due on 24 December 2025, and the ebook uses that day as the lease start. A receipt was not in the filing. If the money was received then, the 99-year clock is already running while HDB’s completion period runs to September 2032.
  • HDB’s plan for Chencharu, reported by The Business Times in June 2024, is about 10,000 homes by 2040, at least 80% of them public. The private home is early. The estate around it is not finished.
  • Facilities in the ebook can be redrawn.
