Fact ledger

Chencharu Central Residences fact ledger: a row without a second source stays Reported.

The name Chencharu Central Residences is Reported, not confirmed. What is confirmed: HDB sold the 99-year leasehold site on 26 September 2025 for $1,012,588,989.89, about $980 psf ppr. Confirmed means HDB, LTA, Gamuda’s exchange announcement, or a named article in The Business Times. The PropNex ebook is an agency guide. Its figures stay Reported even where other agent sites repeat them.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026
