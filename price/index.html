Chencharu Central Residences / Price

Price

There is no developer price list yet.

No developer price list has been released as of 18 November 2026. The land sold for $1,012,588,989.89, about $980 per square foot per plot ratio. A PropNex agency ebook prints guide prices from about $1.3 million for a two-bedroom. That is an agency guide, not a developer price list.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

The tender

HDB awarded the Chencharu Close mixed commercial and residential site on 26 September 2025 to Evia MCS Pte Ltd, Gamuda (Singapore) Pte Ltd and H108 Pte Ltd. The Business Times described that consortium as entities of Evia Real Estate, Gamuda and Ho Lee Group. Confirmed

Gamuda’s 29 September 2025 filing states the bid as $979.84 psf ppr, using a maximum gross floor area of 96,007 square metres. HDB’s award note has been quoted at 96,008 square metres. Dividing the award price by either area still rounds to about $980, which is the rate The Business Times printed. Confirmed

What the agency guide prices mean

The PropNex ebook, file-dated 16 November 2026, prints a star beside “guide price, subject to change”. It does not print a psf, a stack, or a storey. Several competing pages now call the same numbers a developer price list. This guide does not. Reported

 Counts are this guide’s sum of the ebook’s nine rows. 228 + 395 + 180 + 72 = 875. The quantum applies to the group, not to every size inside it.

The ebook prints each figure with its second decimal hidden, for example “$1.3x million”. Read strictly, that is a figure from $1.30 million to $1.39 million. On the 527 sq ft two-bedroom, $1.30 million is about $2,467 psf and $1.39 million is about $2,638 psf. That arithmetic is only a way to read an incomplete number. It is not a forecast. Estimate

Older analyst comments

On 11 September 2025, the day the tender closed, Knight Frank’s Leonard Tay told The Business Times the residences “could possibly be above $2,300 psf”. CBRE’s Tricia Song pointed at Parktown Residence in Tampines, the last similarly equipped site: 1,041 of 1,193 homes, 87%, at an average $2,360 psf on its February 2025 launch weekend. Those sentences are a year old. They are not a 2026 price list. Confirmed

A September 2025 comment and a November 2026 guide quantum can both be in the market at once. They are not the same kind of number. Wait for the developer’s list before treating either as the price you will pay.
