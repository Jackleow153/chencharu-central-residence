Where this stands

The podium is the product. The price is not proven yet.

HDB sold this as the first private-housing site in the new Chencharu precinct, and required a bus interchange and a hawker centre on it. That is a different building from a normal condominium plot.

What is not proven is the selling price. A PropNex ebook dated 16 November 2026 prints guide quanta from $1.3x million. The “x” means the next digit is not stated. It is not a developer price list, and several agent sites are already presenting it as one.

“Developer price list” from $1.3x millionThe PropNex ebook calls these guide prices, subject to change. No developer price list was found. Reported
Exactly 875 homesHDB’s tender estimate was 875 and it said the developer’s number may vary. In September 2025 Evia’s managing partner told The Business Times the scheme included about 860 apartments. The ebook now says 875. Reported
Evia, Gamuda and Ho Lee as equal developersHDB’s tenderers are Evia MCS, Gamuda (Singapore) and H108. Gamuda’s filing gives Gamuda (Singapore) 50%, Evia MCS 30% and H108 20% of the two land companies. Ho Lee Group holds 30 of H108’s 100 shares. Confirmed
TOP in 2029 or 2030HDB set a project completion period of 84 months from acceptance of the tender on 26 September 2025, which runs to September 2032. That is not a published residential TOP date. Confirmed
A measured walk of under 6 minutes, or 330–350 mGamuda’s filing says within 450 metres of Khatib. The ebook says under 6 minutes and fully sheltered. Another site says about 330 to 350 m. This guide did not survey the path, and it does not turn minutes into metres. Reported
