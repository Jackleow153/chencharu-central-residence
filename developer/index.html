Chencharu Central Residences / Developer

Developer

Three tenderers. The filing sets the shares.

The successful tenderers on HDB’s award are Evia MCS Pte Ltd, Gamuda (Singapore) Pte Ltd and H108 Pte Ltd. Gamuda’s exchange filing then says who owns the two companies that take the land.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

Who holds the land

Gamuda Berhad’s announcement of 29 September 2025 says the letter of award is dated 26 September 2025, and the land will be acquired through Polaris Times Square Residences Pte Ltd and Times Square Mall Pte Ltd. Both companies were incorporated on 23 September 2025. Each has the same shareholders. Confirmed

 Gamuda Berhad, 29 September 2025. Out of 100 shares in each company. Confirmed

Evia MCS was incorporated on 29 August 2025. Its shares are held by Evia Real Estate (12) Pte Ltd, 2 of 3, and MCSL Pte Ltd, 1 of 3. H108 was incorporated on 17 March 2025. Its 100 shares are held by Atad Investment, 30, Ho Lee Group Pte Ltd, 30, Ideal Plumbing Holding, 20, and OES Holdings, 20. Ho Lee is a shareholder of H108. It is not a one-third partner in the land companies. Confirmed

The same announcement says the fresh 99-year lease starts when the government receives the full price, and that the lease does not include the bus interchange and hawker centre land. It also said the scheme was not final: up to 875 homes, and a preliminary main construction cost of about $448 per square foot excluding land. That figure is a cost comment, not a selling price. Confirmed

The ebook’s line “Evia Real Estate, Gamuda Land and Ho Lee Group” is the market name for that consortium. The Business Times used the same description. It is not the share table. Reported

What The Business Times recorded about their other work

Evia’s managing partner Vincent Ong told The Business Times that the same three parties developed the OLA executive condominium in Sengkang, completed the year before the tender, and that Evia and Gamuda developed Gem Residences in Toa Payoh. He also said the Chencharu scheme they had in mind was about 860 apartments plus a retail mall at street level and basement 1. Confirmed

About 860 was a tender-time description. The ebook, a year later, says 875, which is also HDB’s estimate. Both stay in the ledger until the developer publishes one number.

Consultants named only in the ebook: P&T Consultants, Tinderbox, Index Design. Reported
