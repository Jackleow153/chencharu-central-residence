Chencharu Central Residences / Floor plans

Floor plans

No official floor plans have been released.

The only unit table in hand is from a PropNex ebook, and it is marked subject to change. It lists 875 homes in nine types, from 527 to 1,389 sq ft. It is not a set of floor plans.

Two-bedroom types are 228 homes, 26%. Three-bedroom types are 395 homes, 45%. Four-bedroom types are 180 homes, 21%. Five-bedroom types are 72 homes, 8%. Three- and four-bedroom types together are 575 homes, about two-thirds of the table. Estimate

What this table cannot tell you

  • There is no plan, no bay window note, no balcony, no facing, and no stack.
  • The 3-bedroom Premium starts at 797 sq ft, which is smaller than the 807 sq ft 3-bedroom. “Premium” here is a name in the ebook, not a proven larger layout.
  • HDB’s own estimate was about 875 homes, and HDB said the number the developer builds may vary. In September 2025 Evia’s managing partner told The Business Times the proposal was about 860 apartments. Reported
  • The ebook’s illustrations are artists’ impressions. The file says they are not the final product.

When a developer brochure exists, check three things before the price: the strata area against this table, which rooms are in the strata area, and whether the study has a window.
