Condo vs BTO

Chencharu condo vs Chencharu BTO: two different purchases in the same estate.

A 4-room flat at Chencharu Acres, HDB’s November 2026 BTO, costs $353,000 to $428,000 before grants. The Chencharu Close condo has no developer price list yet; an agency ebook prints guide prices from about $1.8 million for a three-bedroom. Most buyers will qualify for only one of the two.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

How to think about it

  • If you qualify for the BTO and the price matters most, the BTO is the far cheaper way into the same estate. A 4-room flat of about 93 sq m costs less than a quarter of the condo’s agency guide for a three-bedroom of 74 to 89 sq m.
  • If you already own an HDB flat past its minimum occupation period, or earn above the BTO income ceiling, the condo is the new home you can buy in Chencharu.
  • If you own private property, a BTO flat is not open to you.
  • The condo’s extra cost buys condo facilities, no minimum occupation period, the interchange and hawker centre downstairs, and a home you can sell or rent on the open market. Whether that is worth the gap depends on the developer’s real price, which is not out yet.
