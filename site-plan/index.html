Chencharu Central Residences / Site plan

Site plan

The site plan that matters is the GFA split.

HDB’s site is 29,450.3 square metres, with a 61-metre height limit. Gamuda’s filing sets the maximum gross floor area at 96,007 square metres. At least 74,507 square metres has to be homes. Shops are capped. A bus interchange and a hawker centre are required.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

What the award documents fix

Site area29,450.3 sq m, about 7.3 acres. Lot 04195V, Mukim 19. Confirmed
Maximum GFAGamuda’s filing says 96,007 sq m, which it converts as 1,033,410.7 sq ft. HDB’s award note has been quoted at 96,008 sq m. The gap is 1 sq m. Minimum GFA in the filing is 86,407 sq m. Confirmed
Plot ratio3.26, stated in Gamuda’s filing and in The Business Times when the tender was launched. Confirmed
Height61 metres Singapore Height Datum on the HDB particulars. Gamuda calls it a technical height control of 61 metres. Confirmed
HomesMinimum 74,507 sq m, which is 801,986 sq ft and about 78% of the maximum. Confirmed
Retail GFAMaximum 12,600 sq m, which is 135,625 sq ft. At least 1,000 sq m net floor area is for a supermarket. The Business Times says the commercial component is one strata lot and cannot be subdivided. Confirmed
Bus interchange and hawker centreMinimum 5,400 sq m for the interchange, including 400 sq m of commercial space in the concourse, and minimum 3,500 sq m for the hawker centre. In square feet those minimums are 58,125 and about 37,674. Added to the housing minimum and the retail cap, they equal Gamuda’s 96,007 sq m maximum. The 400 sq m is already inside the 5,400. Confirmed
Lease of the interchange and hawker landGamuda’s 29 September 2025 announcement says the 99-year lease does not include the land those two uses sit on. Confirmed

What is only in the ebook

The ebook says 875 homes in 11 residential blocks of 12 storeys, an air-conditioned bus interchange, a 40-stall hawker centre of about 720 seats, and a mall of supermarket, retail and food under the homes. It names architect P&T Consultants, landscape Tinderbox and interior Index Design. None of that is in the HDB award note. Treat it as the current marketing scheme. Reported

Twelve storeys under a 61-metre cap is plausible. It is not proof. A podium for buses, stalls and shops will take a share of that height.

There is no dimensioned site plan in the ebook. The map in it is a location sketch, and the file says the drawings are artists’ impressions.
