Chencharu Central Residences / Schools

Schools

Names are published. Walking distances are not.

No source checked for this guide gives a measured distance from the site boundary to a school gate. A primary-school distance priority is a MOE rule applied to a finished address. This project has no postal address yet.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

Named by PropNex research in The Business Times

On 11 September 2025 Wong Siew Ying, PropNex head of research, listed these as near the Chencharu Close plot. The article does not say “within 1 km”. Confirmed

  • Peiying Primary School
  • Naval Base Primary School
  • Naval Base Secondary School
  • Chung Cheng High School (Yishun)
  • Orchid Park Secondary School
  • Yishun Innova Junior College

Added by the ebook, not in that Business Times list

The ebook also names Northland Primary. It does not give metres. Reported

Do not plan a Primary 1 application on an agency map. When a house number exists, check MOE’s distance tool for that year. School names and programmes change.
