Chencharu Central Residences / Showflat

Showflat

There is no confirmed showflat address.

The PropNex ebook does not name a gallery, a road, or an appointment date. One Huttons page, updated 15 November 2026, says a showflat along Yishun Ring Road opposite Wisteria Mall, and marks both the address and the dates TBC.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

What is actually said

PropNex ebook, 16 Nov 2026No showflat street. Launching end of January 2027. Reported
lihyuan.com, updated 15 Nov 2026Yishun Ring Road, opposite Wisteria Mall. Preview 16 January 2027 and booking day 30 January 2027, both marked TBC. Reported
TeeCo, October 2026Preview start estimated 16 January 2027. Prices not announced. Reported
PropertyNet, 13 Nov 2026Preview date not announced. Reported

Three write-ups in the same month do not agree. This guide will not pick 16 January as the date.

What to ask for when a gallery does open

  • The developer price list, not a guide quantum.
  • Floor plans with strata area, for the type you are comparing with the ebook table.
  • Which block faces the bus interchange and the hawker centre, and which faces the other way.
  • Whether the 40 stalls and the mall are in the same strata you are buying. The retail cap is one lot and cannot be subdivided. You are not buying a shop. Confirmed

Register if you want a message when a street address is actually published. A form does not hold a unit.
