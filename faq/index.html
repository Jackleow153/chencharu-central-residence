Chencharu Central Residences / FAQ

FAQ

Short answers. The ledger has the sources.

This is not the official project website. HDB sold the land. A developer price list was not found on 18 November 2026.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

When does it launch?

The PropNex ebook says the end of January 2027. It does not name a booking day. A 16 January preview and a 30 January booking day are one agent’s TBC dates, and other write-ups do not share them. Reported

What is the price?

No developer price list. The land was $1,012,588,989.89, about $980 psf ppr. Ebook guide quanta start at $1.3x million for a two-bedroom group. The x is not a digit you can take to the bank. Reported

Who is the developer?

HDB awarded the site to Evia MCS Pte Ltd, Gamuda (Singapore) Pte Ltd and H108 Pte Ltd. Gamuda’s filing gives Gamuda (Singapore) 50%, Evia MCS 30% and H108 20% of Polaris Times Square Residences and of Times Square Mall. Ho Lee Group holds 30 of H108’s 100 shares. It is not a one-third partner. Confirmed

How many homes?

HDB estimated 875 and said the number may vary. Evia described about 860 apartments to The Business Times in September 2025. The ebook now says 875 in 11 blocks of 12 storeys. Reported

How far is the MRT?

Khatib is NS14. Yishun and Northpoint City are one stop. Orchard is 8 stops. Raffles Place is 12 stops, not 13. Confirmed Gamuda’s filing says within 450 metres, and the ebook says a sheltered walk under 6 minutes. This guide did not measure the path. Reported

Where is the showflat?

Not stated in the ebook. One agent page says Yishun Ring Road opposite Wisteria Mall and marks it TBC. Reported

When is TOP?

No TOP date was found. HDB’s project completion period is 84 months from 26 September 2025, which runs to September 2032. Confirmed

Do I pay more through an agent?

On a normal Singapore developer sale the list price is the same and the developer pays the agent. The Chencharu developer has not published that sentence. Treat it as the market practice, not as a term of this project, until the appointment letter says it. Reported

Is the mall for sale as shops?

The Business Times reported that the commercial space is capped and must be held as a single strata lot. Buyers of homes are not buying strata shops. Confirmed
