Chencharu Central Residences / Facilities

Facilities

Chencharu Central Residences facilities: separate the podium HDB required from the ebook’s club list.

A bus interchange, a hawker centre and commercial space are conditions of the land. The 50-metre pool, the gym and the courts are a list in the PropNex ebook, marked subject to change.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

On the land sale

  • Bus interchange, minimum 5,400 sq m. That minimum includes 400 sq m of commercial space in the concourse. Confirmed
  • Hawker centre, minimum 3,500 sq m. Confirmed
  • Commercial space, maximum 12,600 sq m. At least 1,000 sq m of net floor area is for a supermarket. The Business Times says this commercial component is one strata lot and cannot be subdivided. Confirmed

The ebook is more specific: an air-conditioned interchange, 40 stalls and about 720 seats, and a mall with shops and food directly under the homes. The stall count and the air-conditioning are Reported. The supermarket minimum is already in the land filing. Reported

Club facilities named only in the ebook

The page is headed “The Ownership Advantage”. It is not a developer facilities schedule. Reported

  • 50 m grand lap pool
  • HYROX-ready gym
  • Indoor air-conditioned racquet hall
  • Padel court and two pickleball courts
  • Boxing ring
  • Yoga and dance studio
  • KTV and movie room
  • Bath house, hot and cold plunge
  • Seven function rooms
  • Pet corner, described as including a pool for pets
  • Putting green, written as 2/6-hole

Until these are in a developer brochure or a written specification, they can be redesigned. Do not choose a stack because a court is drawn next to it in an agency ebook.
