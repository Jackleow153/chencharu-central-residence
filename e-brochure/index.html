Chencharu Central Residences / Ebook

Ebook

An agency guide, not the developer brochure.

The file is a seven-page PropNex ebook titled Chencharu Central, Great Location Edition. Its properties date it 16 November 2026. The cover says launching end January 2027. The disclaimer says the drawings are artists’ impressions and not the final product.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

What was taken from it

  • The marketing name, the nine-row unit table, and the four guide quanta. Reported
  • 11 blocks of 12 storeys, the 40-stall hawker note, and the club-facility list. Reported
  • The under-6-minute walk, kept as a claim and not converted into metres. Reported
  • The precinct figures that match HDB’s 12 June 2024 plan: about 70 hectares, about 10,000 homes by 2040, the 400-metre bus-only road, and the bungalow at 50 Bah Soon Pah Road kept for community use in a new park. Confirmed
  • The ebook’s “about 5,200 flats from five BTO projects” was not used as a total. Reported
  • HDB had launched four BTO projects by 11 December 2025, and launched Chencharu Acres in November 2026. Confirmed

What was not taken from it

  • “13 stops to Raffles Place.” The count is 12.
  • A North-South Corridor opening from 2029 that cuts journeys by up to 30 minutes. LTA’s September 2026 timeline is different, and the 30 minutes is not used.
  • Any illustration, presented as the building.
  • The slogans on the cover. They are not facts.

The PDF is not hosted here. When the developer issues a brochure, this page should be replaced with that file’s name, date and contents.
