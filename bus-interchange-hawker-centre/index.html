Chencharu Central Residences / Bus interchange

Bus interchange

Chencharu bus interchange and hawker centre: what HDB fixed, and what is still a drawing.

A new bus interchange and a hawker centre are conditions of the land at Chencharu Close. HDB’s sale requires at least 5,400 square metres for the interchange and 3,500 square metres for the hawker centre, under the condo’s homes and shops. No separate completion date has been published for either.

By Jack Leow, CEA R074023ILast checked 18 November 2026

What the land sale fixes

Bus interchangeAt least 5,400 sq m, about 58,125 sq ft, including 400 sq m of commercial space in the concourse. Confirmed
Hawker centreAt least 3,500 sq m, about 37,674 sq ft. Confirmed
ShopsAt most 12,600 sq m, with at least 1,000 sq m net for a supermarket. One strata lot that cannot be subdivided. Confirmed
HomesAt least 74,507 sq m of the maximum 96,007 sq m GFA. About 875 homes. Confirmed
LeaseThe condo’s 99-year lease does not include the land the interchange and hawker centre sit on. Confirmed

Sources: Gamuda Berhad’s announcement of 29 September 2025; The Business Times, 11 September 2025, for the single strata lot. Full rows in the fact ledger.

What is only in the marketing so far

The PropNex agency ebook describes an air-conditioned interchange and a 40-stall hawker centre of about 720 seats, with a mall of supermarket, shops and food under the homes. None of that detail is in the HDB award. Treat it as the current scheme, not a commitment. Reported

When will the Chencharu bus interchange be ready?

No date has been published for the interchange or the hawker centre on their own. HDB’s tender gives the whole project a completion period of 84 months from the award on 26 September 2025, which runs to September 2032. Confirmed That is the outer limit for the development, not an opening date for the buses.

HDB’s 2024 plan for the Chencharu estate also includes Chencharu Link, a 400-metre bus-only road running north to south through the new estate. Confirmed

A Yishun integrated development: what it means day to day

  • Buses and food downstairs. Residents will live over an interchange, a hawker centre and a supermarket, within 450 metres of Khatib MRT. Confirmed
  • Footfall and noise. An interchange and a hawker centre bring buses and crowds from the whole new estate. When floor plans are out, check which blocks face the interchange and the hawker centre.
  • You are buying a home, not the podium. The shops are one strata lot and the interchange and hawker land sit outside the condo’s lease. Ask the developer how the costs of the podium are shared before you buy.
  • Price. The land rate of about $980 psf ppr reflects these uses. The price page sets out what is known.
